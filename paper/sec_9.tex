\section{Scope and limitations}
\label{sec:scope}\label{sec:honest}

\subsection{What is established, and its limits}
\label{sec:scope:declared}

%
%
%
%
%
What the work establishes is four things, each with its measurement attached: one sampling primitive that targets four frequency-resolved channels, of which the single-particle ones are reconstructed here from ranked or sampled subspaces and the two neutral ones are full-sector references (Table~\ref{tab:provenance}); a
two-sided bound on the error of the reconstruction, whose leakage term is a posteriori, evaluated at
the operating fractions at five sizes up to a sector of dimension $10\,306\,296$, with its
non-vacuity threshold, and the true error at its crossing of the trivial bound,
${\approx}6\times10^{-3}$ to within a factor of three, measured at $L=6$--$12$
(Sec.~\ref{sec:frontier}); an
obstruction showing that no orbital-rotation invariant can follow that bound along a fiber of fixed
invariants (Sec.~\ref{sec:nogo}); and three prices---support, resolution and shots---each measured here (Sec.~\ref{sec:prices}).

\paragraph{Limitations.} Four limits apply to all of it. The bound needs the exact ground state
(hypotheses H1 and H4 of Theorem~\ref{thm:leakage}), so it is a certificate for benchmarking where
exact diagonalization is available, not a diagnostic that a shot-based pipeline can evaluate on its
own (Sec.~\ref{sec:leakage:branches}). On the subspaces at which the resource scan meets its accuracy
target the bound is no better than the trivial one, at all five sizes; it becomes informative only
above those sector fractions (Sec.~\ref{sec:frontier:threshold}). Every system is classically
tractable, one-dimensional Hubbard rings at $L\le14$ and nineteen molecular active spaces, and no
quantum advantage is claimed (Sec.~\ref{sec:scope:boomerang}). Every reported result rests on
classical sampling, Born draws of exactly time-evolved probes or their infinite-shot ranking, and the
noise results are controlled simulations (Sec.~\ref{sec:scope:hw}).

The momentum-resolved spectra are one-dimensional Hubbard rings at $L\le12$ with $\eta=0.10$--$0.18\,t$ (the support, certificate and shot measurements reach $L=14$), which is the
exact-diagonalization convention for this observable---Xu, Lu, Tohyama and Shao compute it at $L=12$
and $\eta=0.2\,t$ at half filling, and at $L=16$ at quarter filling, in the extended
Peierls--Hubbard chain~\cite{xu2024}---but the resolution criterion the dynamical-DMRG literature
applies at those sizes is not met here, so Sec.~\ref{sec:physics} rests its spin--charge statement on
pole positions at four sizes rather than enlarge $\eta$.

\subsection{Further results, in the supplementary material}
\label{sec:scope:further}

% 2026-11-14 (closing round 2, D2): the seven molecules are picked out by what makes their test genuine -- the

% molecules have sectors of 20 to 300 determinants and five of them (BH, BeH2, H2O, H4: 24; C2: 90) are exact

Five further results are stated in full in the supplementary material. No orbital-rotation invariant
of the state, the efficiently measurable monotones of fermionic non-Gaussianity included, can follow
the boundary leakage: an invariant is constant along orbital-rotation fibers on which the leakage
moves, and the one bound invariants supply is vacuous by a factor that grows with the system
(Sec.~\ref{sec:nogo}). The method has three prices, support, resolution and shots: the
determinant support that reconstructs $A(\omega)$ to relative $L_1<0.05$ at $\eta=0.15\,t$ grows as
$e^{1.05\,L}$ over $L=4$--$14$, %% 2026-11-08 (JCP, pass 7): unsourced shot-frontier numbers withdrawn here; verbatim in
%
and the finite-shot budget exceeds the support it buys
(Sec.~\ref{sec:prices}). The full-sector references reproduce the opposite finite-size scaling of
the charge and spin gaps of the Hubbard chain: the charge gap decreases to within $6.2\%$ of its
Lieb--Wu value at $L=12$, while the spin gap closes as $\approx2/L$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:physics}). Under
a synthetic bit-flip channel, configuration recovery improves on no recovery from the same
corrupted bitstrings, increasingly with the noise; on the seven molecules of the nineteen-molecule
suite whose sampled subspace does not contain the probe's Krylov space (in the other twelve it does,
so the reconstruction is exact by construction), the sampling primitive reproduces the orbital-projected $A(\omega)$, a consistency check outside the
Hubbard family (Sec.~\ref{sec:scope:hw}). The bond dimension that tracks the determinant support is
the cost parameter of a matrix-product state, and the largest minimal bond dimension over the
targets measured here is $42$; on an emulated, under-sampled $\mathrm{N_2}$ head a classical
Epstein--Nesbet prior supplies the largest available improvement (Sec.~\ref{sec:scope:boomerang}).
