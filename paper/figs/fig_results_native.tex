\caption{\textbf{The two-sided bound against the true error, on Born-ranked subspaces of the
$L=8$ ring (classical simulation, $\eta=0.18\,t$).} As the sector fraction $\lvert S\rvert/D$ grows,
the missed weight $1-w$ (triangles), which is the lower bound of Theorem~\ref{thm:leakage}, falls
toward zero, the true relative $L_1$ error (filled circles) falls from $0.40$ to
$1.2\times10^{-6}$, and the smaller leakage branch of the upper bound (open squares; first-order up to
$\lvert S\rvert/D=0.60$, second-order from $0.70$ on) falls from $20$ to $3.8\times10^{-4}$; the bound
is the smaller of that branch and the trivial branch $1+w$ (dotted). Left of the
vertical line, $\mathrm{FR}^{\ast}=0.74$, the bound is no better than the trivial one (shaded),
although the true error is below $0.05$ (dashed line) from $\lvert S\rvert/D\approx0.525$ on; the
bound itself first falls below $0.05$ between $0.90$ and $0.92$. Star: the probe's own determinant
support $S^\ast=\mathrm{supp}(\phi)$, where $w=1$ exactly ($\lvert S\rvert/D=0.571$); its error,
$0.35$, is ten times that of the Born-ranked subspace of nearly the same size ($0.560$), whose
captured weight is lower ($0.9988$). The subspaces are the infinite-shot limit of the Born ranking
(Sec.~\ref{sec:frontier}); the reconstruction is the Krylov reconstruction at a declared depth, except
for the last three points, which use the dense Rayleigh--Ritz spectrum of $H_S$.
Data: \texttt{data/c3\_frontier/C3\_grid.json}, \texttt{data/c3\_frontier/second\_order/cells.json},
\texttt{data/c3\_frontier/second\_order/v2.json}, \texttt{z\_fine\_L8.txt} and \texttt{support\_witness.json} of the deposit.}\label{fig:results}
